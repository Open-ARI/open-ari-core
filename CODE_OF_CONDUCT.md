# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical disagreements with
evidence. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and disclosure of private personal
information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban participants
whose conduct harms the community. Apply these rules consistently and explain
moderation decisions when doing so does not expose private information.

Report conduct concerns privately to Shaun Murphy through the contact options
on https://github.com/shoon. Do not post another person's private information
in an issue. A maintainer involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome
alone; seek an uninvolved maintainer when available.
